Public Health Priorities in Pakistan

🦟 Vector-borne

Dengue and malaria can rise in the rainy season.

💧 Water & food-borne

Typhoid, hepatitis and diarrhoea are linked to unsafe water and hygiene.

🫁 Respiratory

TB and childhood pneumonia are treatable when found early.

🩺 Chronic conditions

Diabetes and high blood pressure often show no early symptoms.

About the creator

Making Public Health Knowledge Accessible to Everyone.

HealthSphere is an educational initiative designed to make disease prevention and public health knowledge easier to understand. Created by Yashaf Mateen, the platform aims to help individuals, families, students, and communities learn about diseases, recognize health risks, and make informed decisions about prevention.
